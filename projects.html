Sahyog Caring Bureau

A caretaker support platform designed to help families find suitable care based on their needs. Families can submit their requirements, discuss care options with the Sahyog team, and get support for elderly care, patient care, recovery, bedridden care, and short or long-term assistance.

The platform also includes a care guide, direct enquiry system, callback support, and a “Talk to Sahyog” assistant with a responsive interface for mobile and desktop.

What It Does

✓ Elderly & Patient Care ✓ Recovery & Bedridden Care ✓ Day & 24-Hour Care ✓ Requirement-Based Enquiry ✓ Direct Inquiry & Callback ✓ Talk to Sahyog Assistant

Tech: HTML, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript, Flask & MySQL
